# Booking FAQ

## Passenger names
The passenger name on a booking must exactly match the name on the passport. Changing a name after booking may be charged or not allowed.

## Cancellation and changes
All demo bookings can be cancelled free of charge within 24 hours. After that, the airline's fare rules apply: basic and saver fares are usually non-refundable, while standard and flexible fares allow changes for a fee plus any fare difference.

## Seats and special requests
Aisle and window seats can be requested at booking. Vegetarian and other special meals must be requested at least 48 hours before departure on full-service airlines; low-cost airlines (easyJet) sell food on board instead. Families with children under 12 are seated together free of charge on most airlines.

## Travel insurance
We recommend buying travel insurance at the time of booking so cancellation cover starts immediately.
