# Airline fare and baggage policies

> Illustrative demo content for the meetup. Real airline rules change often; always check the airline's website.

## British Airways baggage and fares
British Airways "Basic" economy fares on short-haul routes include one cabin bag and one small under-seat item but no checked bag. Adding a 23kg checked bag during booking costs roughly £35-£65 each way; adding it at the airport costs more. Standard economy and above include one 23kg checked bag. Long-haul economy always includes one 23kg checked bag. Seat selection is free 24 hours before departure for Silver and Gold members of the British Airways Club. Avios are earned on all BA fares.

## easyJet baggage and fares
easyJet fares include one small under-seat bag only. Large cabin bags and 15kg/23kg hold bags must be purchased. Families should add hold bags at booking time: a 23kg bag costs about £30-£50 per flight online but significantly more at the airport. Children under 2 travel on a lap for a fixed infant fee. easyJet does not offer business class and has no frequent flyer alliance.

## KLM baggage and fares
KLM economy "Standard" fares within Europe include one 23kg checked bag; "Light" fares are hand baggage only. Long-haul economy includes one 23kg bag. KLM is a SkyTeam member, and flights earn Flying Blue miles. KLM operates many connections from Scottish airports (Edinburgh, Glasgow, Aberdeen) to Amsterdam Schiphol, which works well for onward connections.

## Loganair baggage and fares
Loganair is a Scottish regional airline. Most fares include one 20kg checked bag and one cabin bag, but aircraft are small (turboprops and regional jets), so cabin bags over 6kg may be gate-checked. Loganair is the main airline serving Dundee Airport, with flights to London City. Loganair flights are often full on weekday mornings; book early.

## Aer Lingus baggage and fares
Aer Lingus "Saver" short-haul fares do not include a checked bag. US-bound flights from Dublin offer US Preclearance, so passengers clear US immigration in Dublin before departure. Aer Lingus flights earn Avios.

## Icelandair baggage and fares
Icelandair economy "Standard" includes one 23kg checked bag. Icelandair offers a free "stopover" in Iceland of up to 7 days on transatlantic tickets, which is popular for seeing the northern lights between September and April.

## Delta, United and Air France long-haul fares
Transatlantic economy fares on Delta, United and Air France include one 23kg checked bag. Basic economy fares cannot be changed and seat selection is charged. Delta and Air France are SkyTeam members (earn Flying Blue); United is a Star Alliance member.

## Alaska Airlines fares
Alaska Airlines is a oneworld member, so British Airways Club members can earn Avios on Alaska flights. Alaska charges for the first checked bag on domestic US flights (about $35-$40).
