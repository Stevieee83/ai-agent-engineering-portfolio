# Destination guides

> Short, illustrative guides used for retrieval-augmented generation in the demo.

## Amsterdam
Schiphol Airport (AMS) to Amsterdam Centraal is about 15 minutes by train; trains run frequently all day. The RAI conference centre is next to Amsterdam RAI station, one stop from Amsterdam Zuid, so hotels around Zuid are ideal for events at the RAI. Canal-belt hotels are charming but pricier. October weather is mild and wet: 10-15°C, bring a waterproof. Cycling is everywhere; watch the bike lanes.

## Reykjavik and Iceland
Keflavik Airport (KEF) is about 45 minutes from Reykjavik by the Flybus. Northern lights season runs from September to mid-April; the best viewing is away from the city lights on clear nights. Iceland is expensive: budget around £20-£30 for a simple meal. The Blue Lagoon is close to the airport and works well on arrival or departure day. Family-friendly: the Perlan museum and whale watching from the old harbour.

## New York
From JFK, the AirTrain plus subway to Manhattan takes about 60-75 minutes; a taxi has a flat fare to Manhattan plus tolls and tip. Midtown hotels are convenient but expensive; Long Island City and Brooklyn offer better value with quick subway links. Autumn (September-November) is a great time to visit.

## Dublin
Dublin Airport to the city centre takes 30-45 minutes by the Airlink bus or Dublin Express coach. Family highlights: Dublin Zoo in Phoenix Park, EPIC The Irish Emigration Museum, and the beach at Howth by DART train. The city is walkable but hotels near the centre book up quickly during rugby weekends.

## Paris
From Charles de Gaulle (CDG), RER B trains reach central Paris in about 35-50 minutes. Families may prefer the Roissybus or a taxi with luggage. Disneyland Paris is served directly from CDG by TGV (about 10 minutes). Many museums are free for under-18s.

## London
From London City Airport (LCY) the DLR reaches Bank in about 22 minutes, making LCY the fastest option for the City and Canary Wharf. Heathrow (LHR) is connected by the Elizabeth line (about 30-40 minutes to central London). The Dundee to London train (LNER) takes about 6-7 hours.

## Seattle
From Seattle-Tacoma (SEA), the Link light rail reaches downtown in about 40 minutes. September and October are usually dry and pleasant. Pike Place Market, the waterfront and the Space Needle are close together.

## Edinburgh and Scotland
Edinburgh Airport (EDI) is Scotland's busiest airport with the widest choice of international flights. From Dundee, the train to Edinburgh Gateway plus a 5-minute tram reaches EDI in about 1h30. Aberdeen (ABZ) is about 1h15 from Dundee by train, then a bus to the airport. Dundee Airport (DND) is small and only 10 minutes from the city centre, with Loganair flights to London City.

## San Diego and Los Angeles
San Diego International (SAN) is only 10 minutes from downtown. Los Angeles International (LAX) can take over an hour to reach downtown or Santa Monica in traffic. A combined California trip often flies into one and out of the other.
