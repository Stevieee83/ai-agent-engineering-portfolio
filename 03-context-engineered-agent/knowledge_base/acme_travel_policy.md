# Acme Analytics Ltd corporate travel policy

> Fictional company policy used in the demo. Applies to travellers who are SUBJECT_TO this policy in the context graph (e.g. Alex Morgan).

## Cabin class rules
Economy class must be booked for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more. Business class is only allowed for flights over 9 hours and requires written manager approval. Basic fares without a checked bag are allowed for trips of 3 nights or fewer.

## Rail first for short trips
For UK journeys under 500 km (for example Dundee to Edinburgh, Glasgow or Aberdeen), rail must be used instead of flying. For Dundee to London, either Loganair from Dundee (DND) to London City (LCY) or the train is acceptable; choose whichever is cheaper.

## Hotel rate caps
Maximum nightly hotel rates (including tax): London £220, Amsterdam £190, Paris £190, Dublin £170, New York £300, Seattle £250, Reykjavik £200, all other cities £150. Hotels must be within 30 minutes of the business venue by public transport.

## Booking window and approval
Book flights at least 14 days before departure where possible. Trips over £1,500 in total need manager approval before booking. The traveller must explicitly confirm the itinerary before any booking is made. Preferred airlines are those that earn the traveller's existing loyalty points, provided the fare is within 10% of the cheapest compliant option.

## Expenses and conferences
Conference travel should arrive the day before the event starts and depart after the event ends (or the following morning if the last flight is before 18:00). Keep all receipts.
