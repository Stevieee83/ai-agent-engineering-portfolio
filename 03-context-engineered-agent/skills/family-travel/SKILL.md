# Family travel

1. **Price for the whole party**: multiply fares by the number of travellers and say so
   explicitly ("£96 x 4 = £384").
2. **Bags**: families almost always need checked bags. Prefer fares that include bags or
   add them at booking time - never at the airport (it's much more expensive). If the family
   has a past trip where they paid extra for bags, mention how this booking avoids that.
3. **Direct flights and sensible times**: prefer direct flights departing between 08:00 and 18:00.
4. **Seating**: note that children under 12 are seated with an adult free of charge.
5. **Hotels**: use `search_hotels` and prefer options with `family_rooms` in the amenities.
6. **Documents**: remind that every child needs their own passport, and mention a consent letter
   if only one parent is travelling.
7. Suggest 2-3 family-friendly activities from the destination guide.
